Purpose and price

The plugin provides reference-based BL14B2 guidance and scientific calculation assistance. The publisher charges no fee and offers no paid purchases or subscriptions through the plugin. AI-platform access remains subject to that platform’s terms.

Scientific and operational use

AI-generated explanations and calculated values can contain errors or be unsuitable for a particular experiment. Check input values, units, assumptions and applicability. The plugin does not operate hardware, approve experiments or replace instructions and decisions from beamline staff.

Information you provide

Only supply information you are authorized to share with the AI platform and JIN server. Review the privacy notice before use. Avoid confidential research information and personal data unnecessary for the calculation.
